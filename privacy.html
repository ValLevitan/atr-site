Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy covers ATR Claude Connector, a private internal tool of ASHER TANNER ROSE, LLC ("ATR", "we").

What data the app uses

The app reads accounting data from ATR's own QuickBooks Online company. This can include accounts, transactions, invoices, bills, customers, vendors and financial reports.

How we use it

We use the data only for ATR's own bookkeeping, reporting and management. The app only reads data. It does not change QuickBooks records.

Where it is stored

The app runs on computers controlled by ATR. Access keys are kept in a private file on those computers. We do not keep a separate copy of the QuickBooks data, except reports that ATR staff choose to save.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent data. We do not share it with anyone, except ATR's own advisors (for example our accountants), or where the law requires it.

Your choices

The QuickBooks company owner can remove the app's access at any time. See the Disconnect page.
