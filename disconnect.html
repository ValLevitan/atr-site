ATR Claude Connector is disconnected

If you see this page, the app no longer has access to the QuickBooks Online company.

To remove access yourself

In QuickBooks Online, open Settings, then Apps (or Manage apps), find ATR Claude Connector and choose Disconnect.

To connect again

Contact management@atr-trust.com. Access is set up again from an ATR computer.
